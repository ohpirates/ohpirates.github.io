A Bold Start!


This week marks the start of a new active stage in the OHPP. At the upcoming meeting on September 3rd, bylaws, meeting dates/frequency, and platform adoption are on the agenda, and we’re hoping to see you there!
Other important events will be discussed as well, such as officer elections and public relations. There will be plenty of chances to have your voice heard, so make sure to be there or be square as they say!


Website Updates


This past month has led to some major updates on the OH pirates website. Here is the changelog posted on 08/29/2026 in the Discord:

  • Fixed article redirects
  • Added social media redirects
  • Updated social media logos to branding compliant images
  • Updated OHPP logo
  • Updated meeting placeholder with signal link

Later in the day after this was posted, an official meeting date was also posted on the home page and projects/events page.


Google Calendar

A google calendar has been published for keeping track of meeting dates and times outside of the Discord server. This will also include any other major events in the state as well as major events on the national level.
Google Calendar


Brand New Newsletter!

If you haven’t already noticed, a shiny new newsletter has been released! New releases will be published on the 1st of each month with any important Party updates and events.
We plan to also have a mailing list in the future for those who are interested!
